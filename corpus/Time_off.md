# Time off

Northstar Studio fictional policy. Owner: People Operations. Version 1, effective July 1, 2026.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Request planned leave at least 10 business days ahead. Your manager approves requests based on team coverage.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager as soon as practical when you are unable to work because of illness. Do not include medical details in a team channel.

## Carryover
Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Use carried days by March 31.
