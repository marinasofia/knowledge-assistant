# Travel and expenses

Northstar Studio fictional policy. Owner: Finance. Version 3, effective August 15, 2026.

## Meal reimbursement
The domestic business travel meal allowance is $75 per day, including tax and tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Submit itemized receipts within 30 days of the expense.

## Booking travel
Book economy flights at least 14 days ahead when practical. Hotel bookings above $220 per night require written manager approval before purchase.

## Expense submission
Submit expenses in the expense portal with a receipt, business purpose, and project code. Finance reviews complete submissions within five business days.
