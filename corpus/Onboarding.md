# New employee onboarding

Northstar Studio fictional checklist. Owner: People Operations. Version 1, effective July 1, 2026.

## First week
Complete security training, confirm payroll information in the approved HR system, and meet your onboarding buddy during the first week. Your manager creates a 30-day onboarding plan.

## Probation review
Schedule a progress discussion with your manager after 30 days and again after 90 days. Record goals and support needs in your private performance workspace.
