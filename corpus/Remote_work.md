# Remote work policy

Northstar Studio is a fictional company. Policy owner: People Operations. Version 2, effective September 1, 2026.

## Work from home
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Coordinate your office days with your manager. Team collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern Time.

## Equipment allowance
Each employee has a $600 home office equipment allowance per calendar year. Get manager approval before buying equipment and submit itemized receipts within 30 days.

## Working abroad
Working from another country requires written People Operations approval at least 20 business days before travel. This policy does not guarantee approval for any destination.
