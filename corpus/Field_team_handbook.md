# Field team handbook

Northstar Studio fictional handbook. Owner: Field Operations. Version 1, effective August 20, 2026. This approved handbook has not been reconciled with the Finance travel policy.

## Meal reimbursement
The domestic business travel meal allowance for field team employees is $90 per day, including tax and tips. The Finance travel policy states $75 per day. Ask Finance to resolve this conflict before submitting a field team meal claim.

## Client visits
Record a visit summary in the project workspace within two business days of a client visit. Include decisions, owners, and the next agreed action.
